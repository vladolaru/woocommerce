# Native WooPayments Workflow Ownership Ledger

This ledger names the Core owner for both behavior halves of every release-critical WooPayments journey. `Initiate` owns the request or merchant/shopper action; `Return/confirm` owns the authoritative response, browser return, provider event, or resulting runtime state.

## Accepted-blocked and manual evidence rule

Before an evidence row can be acknowledged as accepted-blocked or manual, its analysis must include a code-level behavior audit naming both the `Initiate` and `Return/confirm` owners and explaining why automation cannot currently prove the journey. Acknowledgement records a known evidence limitation; it cannot replace the ownership audit.
